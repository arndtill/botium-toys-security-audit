# Scope and Goals of the Audit

## 1. Project Overview

This project is based on the Botium Toys security audit activity from the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

The objective is to assess the organization's existing security controls, review its compliance practices, identify potential risks and recommend improvements to its overall security posture.

This is an educational assessment based on the information provided in the original case study. No direct access to Botium Toys' systems or infrastructure was obtained.

## 2. Audit Scope

The scope of this audit covers the entire security program at Botium Toys.

The assessment includes the organization's assets, employee equipment and devices, internal network, systems, existing security controls and compliance practices.

### Assets Managed by the IT Department

The following assets are included in the assessment:

- **On-premises Equipment:** Equipment used for in-office business operations.
- **Employee Equipment:** Desktops, laptops, smartphones, remote workstations, headsets, cables, keyboards, mice, docking stations, surveillance cameras and other related devices.
- **Storefront Products:** Products available for retail sale on-site and online, stored in the company's adjoining warehouse.
- **Systems, Software and Services:** Accounting, telecommunication, database, security, e-commerce and inventory management systems.
- **Internet Access:** Internet connectivity required for business operations.
- **Internal Network:** The organization's internal network infrastructure.
- **Data Retention and Storage:** Business data stored and maintained by the organization.
- **Legacy System Maintenance:** End-of-life systems that require human monitoring and maintenance.

## 3. Audit Goals

The primary goal is to assess existing assets and complete the controls and compliance assessment checklists to determine which security controls and compliance best practices need to be implemented.

The assessment aims to:

1. Identify and review the organization's existing assets.
2. Evaluate the security controls currently in place.
3. Identify missing or insufficient security controls.
4. Assess compliance with relevant standards and regulations.
5. Determine potential risks to organizational assets and business operations.
6. Recommend improvements to strengthen Botium Toys' security posture.

## 4. Assessment Areas

The assessment will cover three main areas:

- **Security Controls:** Administrative/Managerial, Technical and Physical/Operational controls.
- **Control Types:** Preventative, Corrective, Detective and Deterrent controls.
- **Compliance Practices:** Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and System and Organization Controls (SOC).

## 5. Assessment Methodology

The assessment will use the information provided in the original case study to:

1. Review the organization's assets and existing security practices.
2. Evaluate the controls assessment checklist.
3. Evaluate the compliance checklist.
4. Identify security gaps and potential risks.
5. Develop recommendations based on the findings.

The assessment also considers the Identify function of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF), particularly the need to identify, classify and manage organizational assets.

## 6. Assessment Limitations

This assessment is based on the information and scenario details provided in the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate activity.

No independent inspection, technical testing, penetration testing, or verification of Botium Toys' actual infrastructure was performed.

Findings and recommendations are therefore limited to the information available in the case study.
