# Scope and Goals

## 1. Project Purpose

The purpose of this security audit case study is to evaluate the hyppthetical security posture of Botium Toys, identify potential risks, and recommend improvements to its security controls.

The assessment focuses on protecting business information, supporting operational continuity, and reducing exposure to cybersecurity threats.

## 2. Organization Overview

Botium Toys is treated as a fictional retail business operating in the toy industry.

For this scenario, the organization is assumed to:

- Operate physiscal retail activities and an online sales channel.
- Process customer and employee information.
- Handle electronic payment transactions.
- Maintain inventory and business records through information systems.

These characteristics establish the working scenario for this educational assessment. They are not presented as verified facts from the original course exercise.

## 3. Audit Scope

The assesment will focus on the following areas:

- __Information Assets:__ Customer records, employee information, business records and inventory data.
- __IT infrastructure:__ Computers, operating systems, networks and bussiness applications.
- __Access Control:__ User permissions, authentication and account management.
- __Data Protection:__ Information handling, encryption and backup practices.
- __Payment Security:__ Risks associated with electronic payment processing.
- __Security Operations:__ Monitoring, incident detection and response capabilities.

## Out of Scope:

- Financial auditing.
- Legal certification or formal compliance attestation.
- Physical penetration testing.
- Active exploitation of vulnerabilities.
- Direct testing of production systems.

## 4. Audit Goals

The main goals of this assessment are:

1. Identify critical business assets and the information they handle.
2. Evaluate existing security controls based on the defined scenario.
3. Identify potential vulnerabilities and associated risks.
4. Assess relevant security and compliance requirements.
5. Prioritize findings according to their potential impact.
6. Recommend practical security improvements.

## 5. Methodology

The assessment will follow the next structured approach:

1. Identify and classify relevant assets.
2. Review security controls and identify potential gaps.
3. Evaluate risks based on likelihood and potential impact.
4. Map relevant findings to applicable security frameworks and requirements.
5. Develop recommendations and document the final assessment.

## 6. Limitations

This project is an educational case study based on a fictional business scenario.

It does not represent an actual security audit, independent verification or penetration test. Findings and recommendations will be based on documented scenario assumptions rather than direct access to a real organization's infrastructure.
