# Asset Inventory

## 1. Overview

This inventory identifies the assets mentioned in the Botium Toys security audit case study.

The inventory is based on the information provided in the original Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate activity. It does not represent an independently verified inventory of the organization's infrastructure.

## 2. Identified Assets

| Asset Category | Identified Assets | Description |
|---|---|---|
| On-premises Equipment | Office equipment | Equipment used to support business operations at the company's physical offices. |
| Employee Equipment | Desktops, laptops, smartphones, remote workstations, headsets, cables, keyboards, mouse and docking stations | Devices and peripherals used by employees for business activities, including remote work. |
| Physical Security Equipment | Surveillance cameras | Cameras used to monitor the company's physical locations. |
| Storefront and Warehouse Inventory | Retail products | Products available for sale at the physical storefront and online, stored in the adjoining warehouse. |
| Business Systems | Accounting, telecommunications, database, security, e-commerce and inventory management systems | Systems, software and services managed by IT to support business operations. |
| Network Infrastructure | Internal network | Internal network used to connect organizational systems and support business activities. |
| Internet Connectivity | Internet access | Connectivity required for access to online services and business operations. |
| Information Assets | Customer payment card information, PII and SPII | Sensitive customer information accepted, processed, transmitted or stored by the organization. |
| Data Storage and Retention | Internally stored business data | Data maintained and stored by the organization, including information held in its internal database. |
| Legacy Systems | End-of-life systems | Older systems that require ongoing human monitoring and maintenance. |

## 3. Inventory Observations

The case study identifies several areas that require further attention:

- **Asset Management:** IT needs to improve its ability to identify and classify organizational assets.
- **Sensitive Information:** Customer payment card information, PII and SPII require appropriate access restrictions and protection.
- **Legacy Systems:** These systems are monitored and maintained, but there is no regular maintenance schedule and intervention methods are unclear.
- **Data Recovery:** The organization does not currently have backups of critical data or disaster recovery plans.

## 4. Inventory Limitations

This inventory reflects only the assets explicitly mentioned in the case study.

The original activity does not provide detailed information about individual devices, hardware specifications, system configurations, network architecture, asset owners or exact data storage locations.

Additional information would be required to produce a complete operational asset inventory.
